Watering frequency varies depending on the type of plant, season, and surrounding conditions. Overwatering is one of the most common causes of plant stress, so it’s important to understand your plant’s specific needs. LeafyLane provides watering guidelines with every purchase, including tips on checking soil moisture and recognizing signs of dehydration or overwatering. Seasonal changes also affect watering schedules, and we recommend adjusting routines during colder months when plant growth slows down.
