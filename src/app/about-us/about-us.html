Our Journey

From a small seedling to a thriving garden community

LeafyLane began in 2008 as a small family-owned nursery with a simple vision: to bring the beauty of nature closer to home. What started with a handful of plants and a big dream has blossomed into one of the region's most beloved garden centers.

Our founder, Emily Greenfield, believed that every home deserves a touch of greenery. Today, we continue her legacy by providing not just plants, but personalized guidance, sustainable practices, and a welcoming space for both novice and experienced gardeners.
