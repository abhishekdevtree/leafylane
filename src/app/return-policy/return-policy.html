Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

1. Overview

At LeafyLane Nursery, we take pride in delivering healthy plants and quality gardening products. Due to the live and perishable nature of plants, our return policy is designed to ensure fairness and plant safety.

2. Eligibility for Returns

  • Returns are accepted only for damaged, defective, or incorrect items.
  • Issues must be reported within 24 hours of delivery or pickup.
  • Proof of purchase and clear photos may be required.

3. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return or refund:

  • Plants damaged due to improper care after delivery
  • Items reported after 24 hours of receipt
  • Clearance or discounted items

4. Refund Process

Once your return request is reviewed and approved, refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days. Refunds will be issued to the original payment method.

5. Replacement Policy

In eligible cases, LeafyLane may offer a replacement instead of a refund, subject to product availability.

6. Shipping & Handling

Shipping charges are non-refundable. If a return is approved due to our error, replacement shipping costs will be covered by LeafyLane.

7. Plant Care Responsibility

We provide basic plant care guidance; however, we cannot guarantee plant health after delivery due to environmental, watering, and sunlight conditions.

8. Policy Updates

LeafyLane Nursery reserves the right to modify this Return & Refund Policy at any time without prior notice.

9. Contact Us

For return requests or questions about this policy, please contact us at support@leafylane.com.
