1. Introduction

LeafyLane Nursery (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your personal information is collected, used, and safeguarded.

This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors, customers, and users who access our website, place orders, subscribe to communications, or otherwise interact with LeafyLane Nursery.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate our business and provide a smooth shopping experience. This information may include:

  • Personal identification details such as full name, email address, phone number, and billing or shipping address
  • Account-related information if you create an account with us
  • Order details, transaction history, and payment confirmation (payment data is processed securely by third-party providers)
  • Customer service communications, including emails and support requests
  • Technical data such as IP address, device type, browser type, and operating system

3. How We Collect Information

Information may be collected directly from you or automatically through your interaction with our website. This includes:

  • Placing an order or submitting a form on our website
  • Subscribing to newsletters or promotional updates
  • Contacting us via email or customer support channels
  • Using cookies and analytics tools that track website usage

4. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for legitimate business purposes, including but not limited to:

  • Processing orders, payments, and deliveries
  • Providing order updates, receipts, and customer support
  • Personalizing your shopping experience and improving website performance
  • Sending promotional offers, newsletters, and updates (with your consent)
  • Detecting fraud, unauthorized transactions, or misuse of our services

5. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data only when there is a valid legal basis, including fulfillment of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate business interests, or your explicit consent.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

LeafyLane Nursery uses cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience and analyze site traffic.

Cookies help us remember your preferences, understand how visitors use our website, and improve functionality. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Data may be shared only with trusted third parties, including:

  • Payment processors for secure transaction handling
  • Shipping and logistics partners to fulfill deliveries
  • Service providers who assist in website operations and analytics
  • Authorities or legal entities when required by law

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements.

9. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption, secure servers, and restricted access protocols, to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Request data portability where applicable

11. Children’s Privacy

LeafyLane Nursery does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If such information is discovered, it will be promptly deleted.

12. Third-Party Websites

Our website may include links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of external sites.

13. International Data Transfers

If you access our services from outside our operating region, your information may be transferred and processed in jurisdictions with different data protection laws.

14. Policy Updates

LeafyLane Nursery reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
